# 🎬 My Favorite Movies and Series  
*By Mark Kevin Sheppard – BSIT 502*

---

## 🕷️ Spider-Man (Film Series)

**Genre:** Action / Superhero  
**Director:** Sam Raimi, Jon Watts  
**Why I Like It:**  
- Strong story about responsibility and sacrifice.  
- Great character growth from Peter Parker.  
- Exciting action scenes and emotional depth.  

**Favorite Quote:**  
> "With great power comes great responsibility."

---

## ❄️ Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World (Anime)

**Genre:** Fantasy / Psychological / Drama  
**Main Character:** Subaru Natsuki  
**Why I Like It:**  
- Deep emotional storytelling and time-loop concept.  
- Excellent character development and tension.  
- The mix of hope, pain, and determination hits hard.  

**Favorite Arc:** Return by Death  

**Characters I Like:**  
| Character | Role | Reason |
|------------|------|--------|
| Subaru Natsuki | Main Protagonist | His persistence despite failures |
| Rem | Supporting Character | Loyalty and compassion |
| Emilia | Main Heroine | Kind-hearted yet strong |

---

## ⏳ 17 Again (Movie)

**Genre:** Comedy / Drama / Fantasy  
**Main Actor:** Zac Efron  
**Why I Like It:**  
- It mixes humor with a strong life lesson about second chances.  
- Fun to watch, but also meaningful.  
- Shows how perspective changes with age.  

**Best Line:**  
> "When you're young, everything feels like the end of the world, but it's not. It's just the beginning."

---

## 💭 Summary

All three stories — *Spider-Man*, *Re:Zero*, and *17 Again* — explore **growth, responsibility, and redemption** in different ways.  
They remind me that every setback is a setup for change and self-improvement.
